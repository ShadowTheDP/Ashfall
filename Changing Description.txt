2026-10-24 19:45
- Summary: maps, impact, hub. Plus the restart bug that was skipping floors.
  1) BUG: R inside a floor called reload_current_scene(), which rebuilt Main,
     showed the title and landed in the hub. Because Game.start_floor()
     advances Profile.best_floor on ENTRY, the door then offered the NEXT
     floor - a retry banked progression, skipped the floor and (after a death)
     recorded a death whose body it then discarded. Main.restart_attempt()
     replays the chosen floor: Game.reset_run() is Game-only state, Profile is
     never touched, the old player is freed, stale projectiles/waves are
     purged and the pending hub transition is cancelled. tests/restart_probe
     covers both the paused retry and the dead retry.
  2) MAPS are no longer a row of rooms. Floors: 5600-8200 px, 5-7 rooms,
     29-42 surfaces (was 4200-6400 / 4-6 / ~15). Three new generator passes
     give the floor a second continuous route:
       _link_gallery  bridges the gap at the height of each room's OWN upper
                      deck (matching both neighbours on one shared height
                      produced a link in 1 gap out of 6; per-neighbour lines
                      give 4.7-7.0 bridged/scaffolded gaps per floor);
       _stack_ladders climbable rungs where a pillar does not already stand;
       _caches        cell caches on decks two tiers above the slab, so the
                      high route pays.
     Every added surface goes through the same _reach() predicate the BFS
     verification uses, plus a new _headroom() guard (a generated surface may
     not become a ceiling over another surface closer than 72 px) and an
     _inside_any() spawn filter. tests/gen_stats asserts the shape over 100
     layouts.
  3) Two real generator defects the audits then caught:
     - _place_pillars left a 23 px crack between the pillar and the next
       room; a 28 px body could not enter it, so falling there was final
       (audit_a3's physical walk). The pillar now leaves a 46 px slot on the
       approach side and sits flush against the next room.
     - generation measured spawn clearance with the room's own tallest body
       (21 px for grunts) while the audits check 25 px (FALLBACK_BOX), so a
       point 24 px under a deck passed generation and failed the audit.
       SPAWN_HALF_MIN now matches, and the two constants are linked by comment.
  4) IMPACT: per-tier shape language instead of size scaling, crit halo,
     kill implosion, directional block shell on the shield arc, boss slam /
     charge / cleave telegraphs. 8 new Juice presets. Nothing covers the
     fight: flash <= 24 px, rings are outlines, vignette centre stays clear.
  5) HUB is a room now: far vault with arches, a light shaft with motes,
     hanging chains and censers, a rose wheel, floor inlay medallions, a
     foreground colonnade whose feet overlap the floor band (so it reads as
     near, not as an obstacle) and stations raised to MID so they read from
     across the room.
  6) PANELS + HUD rebuilt on a proportional grid with plates, a segmented
     health bar, tally-based floor progress and timer arcs for combo / EDGE.
