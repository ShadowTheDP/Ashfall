       _link_gallery  bridges the gap at the height of each room's OWN upper
                      deck (matching both neighbours on one shared height

2026-10-24 (bug-hunt + art pass on the committed map/hub work)

- Summary: two independent static reviews of the combat / enemy / generator
  code, then every provable finding fixed and verified. The headline is that
  the FINAL BOSS could not damage the player on any real floor, and the whole
  test suite missed it because the boss was only ever tested in the arena
  fixture (where the floor is at y = 0).

  Bugs found and fixed
  1) P0 WARDEN SLAM DEALT NO DAMAGE. projectile.gd setup_wave() ended with an
     ABSOLUTE `position.y = -16.0`, which overwrote the y computed by the
     caller. On a generated floor (slab y 54..150) that put both shockwaves
     10..106 px above the player's head - and the waves are the slam's ONLY
     damage source (the slam branch never enables the boss hitbox). Fixed at
     the call site (enemy.gd passes the -16 offset in the vector) and the
     absolute write is gone. Regression: tests/boss_probe.tscn (4 seeds).
  2) FunctionWave - every weapon's finisher projectile - ignored world
     geometry: mask was ENEMY only, so the third hit of a combo killed an
     enemy standing on the far side of a pillar. Now masks ENEMY | WORLD and
     bursts on any body without take_hit.
  3) The climb hop and the anti-stuck hop were DEAD CODE: both were gated on
     `state == CHASE`, but the state machine runs _tick_chase from IDLE too, so
     a body that had never been hit never qualified. Added _is_walking()
     (IDLE or CHASE). Regression: tests/climb_probe.tscn.
  4) Four of six enemy kinds could not follow the player onto the gallery,
     which turned the high route into a free safe zone while the door waited
     for every body to die. Added a climb hop (>40 px above, <220 px across,
     cooldown-gated).
  5) The Ram telegraphed from 300 px but its lunge covers ~220 px, so it
     charged into empty space every time. attack_range 300 -> 210.
  6) The finisher wave's damage was a hardcoded 8.0. It now comes from
     data/weapons.json (`wave_damage`, = finisher damage x 0.33 per weapon),
     so a Fractal Edge finisher is not the same as a Rusty Sword one. The
     Aegis counter was also passing attr = 0 to Damage.compute; it now passes
     the tactics level.
  7) level_gen.gd computed a reachability verdict and NOBODY READ IT: _verify
     only push_warning()ed, so a layout with a deck outside the jump budget
     still carried enemy spawns and could ship as a dead floor. generate() now
     retries up to 8 seeds until the layout verifies.
  8) _take_point()'s cramped-room fallback returned the point NEAREST the
     player (it reassigned its answer every loop iteration over a
     farthest-first list), i.e. it spawned the enemy on top of the player -
     the exact outcome the function exists to prevent. Now returns the head of
     the list. audit_v1 asserts it directly.
  9) A GlobPool outlived its floor. It is parented to fx_root (= world), so
     clearing a floor with a glob still ticking carried a damaging puddle into
     the hub. Added to group "hazard_pool" and purged with the other world FX.
  10) J on a station while a panel was already open left the player frozen
     (audit_a4 caught it: "skill left the player running"). The unfreeze was
     in Hub, which cannot see whether the owner actually raised anything; the
     owner now closes the station it refused.
  11) Returning to the hub leaked two CanvasLayers per visit (Archive +
     SkillTree were rebuilt without freeing the old pair). audit_v3 measured
     it and only printed the number; it now asserts it.
  12) Two earlier fixes kept: R inside the hub no longer reloads to the title;
     the reward budget decrements inside the deferred call so two rooms
     clearing in one frame cannot pay one boon for two rooms.

  Art / readability
  - ENEMY SILHOUETTE. Every enemy's body fill and its hollow interior were
    MathArt.STONE - the exact value the floor is drawn in - so a grunt walking
    on stone lost its mass and read as a faint outline on a flat plane. Bodies
    are now PALE with an INK core: bright ring around a dark core is legible
    against floor, vault and player.
  - Slash trail: 4 dashes at 1.2-2.6 px were a hairline the eye could miss.
    Now 2.0-4.2 px with a 1 px white core on the newest dash and three shed
    sparks. Still four separated dashes, so it still cannot enclose a shape.
  - Floor vault (`level.gd`): the far wall stopped at y=60 while the walkable
    line is BASE_Y=150, leaving a 90 px stripe of bare background directly
    above the floor - the room read as a slab floating in front of a black
    hole. The wall now reaches the floor line, carries a 6-band value
    gradient, staggered masonry joints, a keystone per bay, and the niches are
    real recesses (lit top lip, inner side shadows, sill) instead of flat

  - Chains: they were a fraction of the whole vault height and therefore all

  - Hub foreground colonnade: moved to the middle of each gap between
