2026-10-23 13:20
- Summary: Milestone 3 - monochrome, motion-first rebuild at 768x480.
  1) Resolution 640x360 -> 768x480 (window 1536x960, integer x2); physics
     raised to 120 Hz with physics_interpolation; camera moved to
     _physics_process with float position and no rounding. Pixel snapping was
     removed on purpose: motion fluidity now beats pixel alignment.
  2) Colour removed entirely. MathArt now defines one greyscale ramp plus
     semantic aliases (PLAYER/ENEMY/BOSS/PICKUP); every hue in scripts, data
     and shaders was flattened to grey.
  3) Player is a SQUARE that tumbles exactly 90 degrees per jump and lands on
     a new face (face index, 0.42 s tumble, 70 ms settle for short hops). The
     square carries a corner notch and an edge pip so the face change is
     readable. No legs, no arms.
  4) Enemies are plain polygons only (triangle/square/hexagon/pentagon/circle);
     all legs, arms and feet removed; a "hand" is a single dot.
  5) Particles: replaced the hand-written simulator with Godot's
     CPUParticles2D via scripts/systems/juice.gd (nine presets, runtime-
     generated 6x6 texture, still zero image assets). Added coverage for
     enemy landing, windup telegraph, projectile expiry, wave spawn and
     magnetised pickups.
- Subagent audits (4 parallel `pi -p --thinking max` scouts):
  output/agent-reports/{sa_feel,sa_anim,sa_oss,sa_audit}.md. Key findings
  adopted: 120 Hz + interpolation; 90-degree tumble with face index; particle
  counts/lifetimes; no ready-made Godot 4 FX addon fits the zero-texture rule
  (so CPUParticles2D instead of an addon); full file:line audit of what to
  change.
- Key files changed: project.godot, scripts/systems/math_art.gd (greyscale
  ramp, smooth drawing, px_* aliases kept as smooth aliases),
  scripts/systems/juice.gd (new), scripts/player/player.gd (tumble, apex hang,

2026-10-23 15:10
- Summary: Milestone 4 - attack feedback overhaul + geometry fixes, driven by
  three max-thinking subagent audits.
  1) Removed the corner notch, edge pip and all enemy eyes. The player is now
     a plain square; the tumble alone communicates the face change (confirmed
     readable by the user).
  2) Fixed the weapon grip: the hand dot moved from inside the body to the
     square's front edge and now mirrors with facing. Blade length moved into
     data/weapons.json (`blade`: 38/40/50) and the hitbox is derived from it,
     so the drawn blade, the slash arc and the damage box share one number.
     SlashFx is now centred on the grip with radius = blade length.
  3) Tiered impact feedback (scripts/fx/impact_fx.gd): tier 0/1/2/3 drives
     hitstop 2/4/6/8 frames, camera shake, zoom punch, directional line sparks
     (8/10/12/16, fanned around the hit normal), an expanding ring and a 1-2
     frame white contact flash. Crits bump one tier; multiple hits in one frame
     do not stack.
  4) New full-screen feedback (scripts/fx/screen_punch.gd on ScreenFxLayer):
     vignette pulse + two 2-frame scanline offsets + camera zoom punch 1.025.
     Never a full-screen invert.
  5) Smear rewritten from a pie wedge to a crescent trail: three nested
     crescents at 42/66/86% of the blade length, only the last ~38% of the

2026-10-23 16:40
- Summary: Milestone 5 - slash reads as speed, not as a shape; flat 2D.
  1) Grip moved to the LOWER half of the square (facing*13, +4) and the sword
     now rests pointing UP (REST_ANGLE -1.30 rad); the swing runs from
     up-back to front-down. Attack angles and the hitbox offset were
     re-authored to match.
  2) Smear rewritten from a filled crescent to DISCONNECTED STRAIGHT STREAKS:
     four radial rows x three tangential segments, each tangent to the blade
     path, plus a 3px leading tip streak and three golden-angle dots. Filled
     arcs at 38-50 px radius always read as a "mooncake"; broken lines read as
     speed.
  3) Pure flat 2D: player and enemies no longer squash or stretch at all.
     `_body_scale()` returns ONE; enemy lean reduced to +/-0.10-0.12 rad. Hit
     deformation is gone; weight comes from rotation, shadow, particles and
     camera.
  4) The Watcher no longer draws a bow: it attacks directly, with a
     contracting ring during windup and three orbiting satellite dots.
  5) Mathematical elements added to impacts: a rotating regular polygon
     inscribed in the expanding ring (5 sides for heavy, 6 for finisher) and
     golden-angle spark spacing.
